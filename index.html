Hearth & Wit

Wholesome games for families and classrooms.

Each app is built with intention. The games ask for a few minutes of mindful focus, and they are at their best played with someone else: a friend, a parent, a classmate. The content is made for the ages who use it.

There are no ads, no accounts, and nothing designed to be hard to put down: no notifications, no rewards for coming back every day. Progress is there when you want to look, not something to chase. Nothing about you or your children is collected, and what an app saves stays on your own device.
