How we make them

Each app is built with intention. The games ask for a few minutes of mindful focus, and they are at their best played with someone else: a friend, a parent, a classmate. The content is made for the ages who use it. There are no ads, no accounts, and nothing designed to be hard to put down: no notifications, no rewards for coming back every day. Progress is there when you want to look, not something to chase. Nothing about you or your children is collected, and what an app saves stays on your own device.

The apps

  • Times Tables Games

    Practice your times tables and division with quick, friendly games. Progress and diplomas stay in the browser, and the games keep working offline.

    Privacy · Accessibility

  • Printable worksheets

    Free printable times table, division, and color-by-number worksheets. Pick the facts, then print, with an answer key if you want one.

    Division worksheets · Color by number · Privacy

  • Flip Wits

    A family party game for the phone on your forehead. Your team shouts clues, you say the answer, and you flip the phone to score. Flip Wits is in testing and not yet on the App Store.
